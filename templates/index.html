How it works

  1. Your message is sent to the Flask route /predict.
  2. The saved TF-IDF vectorizer turns the text into numbers.
  3. The saved Logistic Regression model predicts SPAM or NOT SPAM.
  4. Flask sends the answer back and this page shows it.
